### 7.2 Explicit Statement on Headline Hypothesis
- **Does A3 beat A0?** **NO, not on graded nDCG@10.** A3 slightly leads A0 on shallow precision metrics (+0.0250 on P@5, +0.0150 on P@10), but trails A0 on nDCG@10 (-0.0558) and MRR (-0.0313).
- **Statistical Significance:** None of the differences between A3 and A0 are statistically significant at $\alpha = 0.05$ (Bootstrap $p = 0.1325$, Wilcoxon $p = 0.2358$). The 95% bootstrap confidence interval for $\Delta \text{nDCG}@10$ spans zero: `[-0.1342, +0.0129]`.
- **A3 vs S0 (Raw Query):** A3 achieves nDCG@10 = 0.6317 vs S0 = 0.6732 ($\Delta = -0.0415$, Bootstrap $p = 0.3140$, Wilcoxon $p = 0.3171$, non-significant).
- **Why A0 remains strong on nDCG@10:** A0 carries all previous turn tokens in a decayed bag. When follow-up queries implicitly rely on incidental words from 2 turns ago, A0's lexical broadness accidentally retrieves background passages that our rubric judges graded as Grade 1. A3's hard title filter strictly enforces focus on the target entity, which eliminates irrelevant hits but slightly narrows graded background recall.

## 11. Cluster Clarifier Decision & Findings

*Evaluated on DEV (30 turns) and confirmed on TEST (40 turns):*

| Split | Clarifier Fired Count | Clarifier FP Rate | Trivial Rule Fired Count | Trivial Rule FP Rate | Outcome |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|---|
| **DEV Split** (n=30) | 23 / 30 turns | **76.7%** | 7 / 30 turns | **23.3%** | Failed to beat trivial baseline |
| **TEST Split** (n=40) | 35 / 40 turns | **87.5%** | 6 / 40 turns | **15.0%** | Corroborates failure to beat baseline |

### Decision:
The cluster-pruning clarifier relies on score margin thresholds between top passage clusters. Because passage scores in dense vector-space distributions frequently differ by $< 0.065$, the clarifier suffered an unacceptable false-trigger rate ($> 76\%$), continually prompting unnecessary user clarification. Per Phase 5.3 rules:
- **The cluster clarifier is DISABLED BY DEFAULT in production (`CONFIG.conversation.clarifier.enabled = false`).**
- The algorithm remains implemented and accessible via explicit options flag (`enableClarifier: true`).
- The Trace Inspector logs `reason: 'Cluster clarifier disabled by default (failed to outperform trivial baseline on DEV)'`.
